# NAWSAQ and DARWAQ — identity case studies

## Brief and decisions

Add two independent fictional projects to the ATHR portfolio, alongside its five existing projects. The new work expands the portfolio into residential development and merchant delivery. These choices demonstrate different applications of identity; they are not presented as a measured ranking of market demand.

- **NAWSAQ / نَوْسَق**: a residential developer concept. “مكانٌ يتّسع لحياتك.” / “Room for your life.” Two offset open architectural frames, generous space, deep blue, pale stone and clay. Applications: residential entrance, project presentation, architectural wayfinding and a static space-exploration interface.
- **DARWAQ / دَرْوَق**: a merchant delivery concept. “كل خطوة واضحة.” / “Every step, clear.” Two forward chevrons, cobalt, orange and direct status language. Applications: van livery, parcels, workwear and a static tracking-interface concept with demonstration data.

Both case studies have original Arabic and English copy, a clear independent-concept disclosure, meaningful localized image descriptions, and links back to ATHR’s real project contact flow. No fictional testimonials, delivery guarantees, commercial results, property specifications or client commissions are asserted. These coined names have not received formal trademark clearance.

## Asset method

Six original 1536 × 1024 raster application photographs were generated with the built-in image generation tool. The two hero images were generated from text. Each follow-up used its corresponding hero as a visual identity reference. Selected images were copied unchanged into the repository. Native SVG marks and interface illustrations were authored separately as scalable website elements. All Arabic and explanatory text remains live HTML, rather than generated image lettering.
