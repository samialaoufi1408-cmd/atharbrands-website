# NAYSAR — fragrance concept for ATHR

> Historical creative record. These project names were replaced by the user-approved names documented in [approved-name-changes.md](approved-name-changes.md). The original image prompts below are retained as history.

Independent portfolio concept, prepared to replace QIRĀ. It is not a client commission or a product offered for sale. No sales results or customer endorsements are claimed.

## Direction

- Arabic name: **نَيْسَار**. Latin name: **NAYSAR**. A coined concept name, without a claim of trademark clearance.
- Message: **حضورٌ يُحَسّ. / Presence, felt.**
- Visual system: an open asymmetric ellipse, generous wordmark spacing, burgundy, soft rose, ivory and silver.
- Applications: the numbered fragrance family, presentation packaging, discovery set, scent card, a product-page design and a campaign composition.
- The Arabic and English website typography remains live text. Text in generated product photographs is restricted to NAYSAR and edition numbers for legibility and consistency.

## Why fragrance

Fragrance broadens ATHR's portfolio beyond hospitality while giving prospective clients concrete examples of identity, packaging and digital applications. Market estimates indicate growth in Saudi online beauty and fragrance sales. These are indicators of commercial opportunity, **not a ranking of demand for branding services**.

Sources reviewed:

- [Mordor Intelligence: Saudi Arabia E-commerce Cosmetics and Fragrances Market](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/saudi-arabia-e-commerce-cosmetics-and-fragrances-market)
- [Monsha'at: E-commerce Programs and Services](https://www.monshaat.gov.sa/ar/ecommerce)

### Hero prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Create one landscape 1536x1024 premium branding portfolio photograph for NAYSAR, a fictional contemporary Saudi unisex fragrance house designed for the ATHR studio portfolio. This is a carefully art-directed, photorealistic commercial still life, not a collage or a presentation board. Show three beautifully proportioned heavy clear-glass perfume bottles with softly squared shoulders, thick bases, pale amber liquid and polished silver cylindrical caps. Each bottle has one minimalist deep oxblood label, with the exact widely spaced uppercase serif brand word NAYSAR and one large collection number, respectively 01, 02, 03. Only those exact words/numbers may appear; no invented fine print, no extra labels, no Arabic text in the photograph. Include two elegant rigid matte oxblood cartons carrying exactly NAYSAR in warm ivory and a thin blind-embossed asymmetric oval motif. Rich tactile materials, a pale rose-ivory studio floor, one low wine-colored platform, a restrained translucent rose acrylic plane, soft directional daylight with credible long shadows and subtle real-glass reflections. A polished product family with ample clean negative space at the upper left, the products occupying the center-right and lower half, harmonious varied heights. Sophisticated editorial beauty photography, clean silhouette, warm luminous highlights, excellent lens detail, no people, no hands, no flowers, no coffee, no hospitality props, no stock watermark, no floating impossible objects. Precise spelling N-A-Y-S-A-R on every branded surface.

### Packaging prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Use the reference only as the exact NAYSAR brand and bottle design reference, and create a NEW landscape 1536x1024 premium editorial packaging photograph with a different composition. Keep the exact clear softly squared thick glass bottle, polished cylindrical silver cap, burgundy rectangular label and widely spaced ivory serif NAYSAR wordmark. Photograph from a high three-quarter angle an open burgundy rigid presentation box with its lid beside it, one NAYSAR 01 bottle nested precisely in a wine-colored die-cut insert, a small slim discovery box containing three elegant glass sample vials, and a clean ivory scent card with only NAYSAR. Off-white textured tabletop, crisp soft morning shadows, tactile blind-embossed asymmetric ellipse on the lid, close attention to physical packaging construction. Luxurious warm but contemporary art direction. The ONLY text permitted anywhere is exactly NAYSAR and the collection numbers 01, 02, 03. Ensure spelling N-A-Y-S-A-R, no invented fine print, no barcode, no human hands, no flowers, no extra brands, no watermark, no collage. This is an independent fictional identity portfolio image, not a real product listing.

Reference: the selected hero image.

### Campaign prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Use the reference as the exact NAYSAR brand and bottle design reference. Create a NEW dramatic landscape 1536x1024 luxury fragrance campaign photograph, not a collage. One NAYSAR 02 perfume bottle only, same thick clear softly squared glass, pale amber liquid, polished silver cylindrical cap, rectangular burgundy label and exact widely spaced ivory serif word NAYSAR above the number 02. Place the bottle at the right half on a low burgundy satin-gloss plinth. Deep oxblood/burgundy seamless background, sculptural side lighting and soft pools of reflected light, one delicate pale rose translucent acrylic curved arc behind the bottle echoing the blind-embossed oval from the reference. Ample clean darker negative space over the entire left half for later website typography. Photorealistic premium editorial fragrance advertising, beautiful glass refraction, crisp believable label and metallic cap. The ONLY text is NAYSAR and 02 on the bottle; no headline, no extra type, no fine print, no logos other than NAYSAR, no watermark, no people, no hands, no flowers. Precise spelling N-A-Y-S-A-R.

Reference: the selected hero image.
